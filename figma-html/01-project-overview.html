01 · PROJECT OVERVIEW

InstaCard

A better way to say hello.

A digital business card experience that keeps contact details and social links together, then makes them easy to share and save without retyping.

Problem

Contact exchange is fragmented.

Paper cards get lost. Social handles are shared separately. Names, phone numbers and email addresses are often retyped by hand.

Product response

One profile. One link. One clear next action.

Create a professional card once, share it in the moment, and let the recipient keep the details they actually need.

01

Create

Add identity, contact details and social channels.

02

Share

Send one profile through a link, QR code or social channel.

03

Save

Keep the contact without manually reproducing the information.

Design principle: Clarity before decoration — reduce friction at the exact moment two people exchange details.
