InstaCard · BCA UI/UX Test
01 · PROJECT OVERVIEW · Case study artifact
The brief, translated into a product problem

Make exchanging identity feel as natural as saying hello.

InstaCard is a digital business-card concept for creating one professional profile that combines contact details and social links, then sharing it without forcing the recipient through a setup task.

Challenge

The exchange moment is fragmented.

Paper cards, manually typed details and separate social handles turn a simple introduction into several small tasks.

Design response

One profile → one share → one save.

The landing page needs to communicate that model quickly enough for a first-time visitor to understand the value without a product walkthrough.

Project frame
