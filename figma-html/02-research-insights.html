Consumer insight hypotheses

These are design hypotheses inferred from the brief and category patterns; they should be validated in future research.

H1

“Don’t make this introduction feel like setup.”

The exchange happens during a live conversation. Any download, account requirement or long form risks breaking the social moment.

Design implication · recipient-first
H2

“I want one identity, not five usernames.”

Contact details and social channels feel more useful when intentionally grouped under one recognizable profile.

Design implication · one profile model
H3

“Professional doesn’t have to mean generic.”

A business card is also a personal-brand surface, so the UI must balance trust with room for visual customization.

Design implication · templates + identity
H4

“The useful moment is what happens after the scan.”

A share is only successful when the recipient can understand, keep and use the details afterwards.

Design implication · save/contact state
