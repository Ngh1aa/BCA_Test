02 · RESEARCH & INSIGHTS

What people actually need when exchanging details.

The research framing is centered on the real exchange moment: speed, recipient effort, identity consistency and trust.

01

Fast enough for a live conversation

The exchange has to take seconds and should never feel like onboarding.

02

No setup for the recipient

Open and save without installing an app first.

03

One identity, multiple channels

Work details and social channels should feel intentionally grouped.

04

Professional but still personal

Templates need structure for trust and flexibility for self-expression.
