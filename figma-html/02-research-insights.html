Evidence before assumptions

I separated what the brief states, what the market shows, and what still needs validation.

The research phase is split into two visible deep dives: Consumer Insight Hypotheses and Competitive Positioning. This keeps the bonus-point reasoning easy to scan instead of hiding it inside a generic research board.

Research stack

Three evidence levels, kept deliberately separate.

01 · Supplied brief

What must be communicated

Simple, Accurate, Multilingual, Integrated, Green and Customized — plus sitemap, user flows and one responsive landing-page direction.

02 · Desk research

What users already see in the category

Digital-card sharing, QR/NFC/link patterns, OCR-based card capture, link-in-bio profiles, contact saving and wallet-style access.

03 · Hypotheses

What I would validate next

Whether no-app receiving, exact contact saving and a three-step Create → Share → Save model are the strongest comprehension drivers.
