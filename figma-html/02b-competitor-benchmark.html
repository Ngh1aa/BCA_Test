Positioning statement

Derived from the comparison, not copied from a competitor.

Opportunity

“A professional identity that moves cleanly from one person’s phone to another.”

InstaCard should feel less like a generic link hub and less like a scanner utility. The distinctive promise is a direct, accurate exchange: create the identity once, share it in the moment, let the recipient save it without retyping.
