Positioning synthesis

The opportunity is the overlap of three useful mental models.

Borrow the category

Digital card

From Bizz Card: users already understand the idea of a digital business identity.

Remove the old mechanism

No OCR / retyping dependency

Against the paper-card capture model: share structured data directly from the source.

Extend the one-link model

One identity → share → save

From Linktree’s aggregation mental model to a more contact-oriented professional exchange.

Positioning hypothesis: InstaCard should feel like a professional identity link that is optimized for the moment of exchange — easier to share than paper, easier to save than scattered social handles.
