03 · UX / PRODUCT STRATEGY

Designing for the moment after “nice to meet you.”

The opportunity is not another digital card. It is a smoother handoff from conversation → identity → saved contact.

Pain point

Paper creates friction

Cards get lost, details need retyping and social links live somewhere else.

Pain point

Contact exchange is fragmented

Phone, email, Instagram and WhatsApp are often shared one by one.

Pain point

First impressions are inconsistent

A strong personal brand online can become generic at the moment of exchange.

Design response

One profile. One link. One clear action to save the contact.

Visual direction: clean · confident · personal.
