From insights to design rules

Reduce friction at the exchange moment — then make the profile worth keeping.

The strategy separates the sender’s need to share fast from the recipient’s need to understand and save. The landing page must communicate both sides without becoming a feature catalogue.

Jobs to be done

One primary job, two complementary actors.

Sender job

When I meet someone, help me share the right version of my identity quickly…

…so I look professional without spelling out contact details or sending several separate links.

Recipient job

When someone shares their card, help me understand who they are and keep the useful details…

…without installing something, retyping information or searching for their accounts later.

Product principles

Four rules used to accept or reject layout/content ideas.

P1

Zero recipient setup

The value proposition must feel usable before signup.

P2

One profile, many channels

Channels belong to the identity; they should not become separate products.

P3

Show product moments

Visual proof beats generic feature icons whenever possible.

P4

One dominant CTA

Early Access remains the main conversion goal across the page.
