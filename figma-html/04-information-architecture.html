Why the extra marketing sections exist

These were intentionally added beyond the minimum feature bullets.

SectionUX uncertainty it resolvesReason for adding it
How it works“What actually happens when I use this?”A digital-card exchange may be unfamiliar; Create → Share → Save supplies a simple mental model before asking for signup.
Card Studio preview“Will this look credible and feel like mine?”The brief includes customization; a live-style product preview turns that feature into tangible proof instead of another claim.
FAQ“Do I need an app? Can I save it? What can I customize?”Late-stage objections can block conversion even after the benefit is understood, so they are answered immediately before the final CTA.
