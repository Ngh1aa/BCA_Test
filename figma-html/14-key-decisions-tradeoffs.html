Design judgment under constraints

The final page is the result of choices — not the accumulation of every possible feature.

For each important decision, I compared viable alternatives, chose the option that best supported the core exchange journey, and accepted a trade-off rather than pretending every goal could be maximized at once.

Five decisions that shaped the page

The strongest senior-level evidence is not the final frame itself, but the reasoning behind what stayed, moved or was removed.

What I deliberately did not optimize for

Scope control is part of product judgment.

Not now

Full feature catalogue

Analytics, CRM depth and team admin could be relevant later, but they weaken the clarity of this first-value story.

Not now

Aggressive motion

Animation is treated as enhancement, not a requirement for understanding the product or completing the CTA.

Not now

Invented proof

No fake conversion uplift, usability score or customer quote is used to make the concept look more validated than it is.

Decision principle: if an idea makes the interface more impressive but makes the product story harder to explain, I remove or postpone it.
