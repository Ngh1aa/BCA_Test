Beyond the landing page

The product experience starts before the share and continues after the contact is saved.

This journey reframes InstaCard as an end-to-end exchange experience, not only a marketing page. It connects sender intent, recipient friction and the product opportunities that should guide future design priorities.

What this changes in the landing page

The page should sell the outcome of the whole journey, not a collection of disconnected features.

Priority 01

Lead with the exchange moment

The strongest pain exists during Share → Open → Save, so these moments deserve the earliest proof.

Priority 02

Design for both actors

Sender polish alone is not enough. Recipient setup, trust and save accuracy are part of the core value proposition.

Priority 03

Keep future value visible

The saved contact is not the end state; reliable reconnection is the longer-term experience the product should protect.

Experience principle: optimize the handoff between people, not just the appearance of the sender’s card.
