GOOD QUESTIONS.

A little clarity,
before you connect.

Here’s what to know about InstaCard.

Does the other person need the app?+

No. The proposed sharing experience lets anyone open your card in a browser and save your details, without installing an app or creating an account.

How do I share my digital card?+

Show your QR code in person, or send your card link by email, message or social media. The recipient opens your card and chooses how to connect.

Can I add my social and messaging links?+

Yes. The planned card supports links such as Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn and WhatsApp alongside your contact information.

Can I make my card look like me?+

Choose a professional template or customize your card. The supplied product concept also includes Premium customization; final features and pricing will be confirmed at launch.

What happens when I request early access?+

At launch, this form will let you request an invitation by email. This is a design preview: no email is submitted or stored, and there is no confirmed launch date yet.
