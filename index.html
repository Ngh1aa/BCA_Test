Quick answers

What should feel clear before you try it?

Does the recipient need to download an app?

No. “No app download required” is one of the six benefits specified in the client brief.

What can I put on my InstaCard?

Your contact details plus links to channels such as Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp.

Can I customize how the card looks?

Yes. The brief specifies professional templates as well as the option to design your own.
