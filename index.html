Quick answers

A few things worth knowing before you share.

Does the recipient need to download an app?

No. A shared InstaCard can open directly, so the recipient does not need to install an app first.

What can I put on my InstaCard?

Your contact details plus links to channels such as Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp.

Can I customize how the card looks?

Yes. Start from a professional template, then adjust the visual style so the card feels like your own.

Can someone save my details after I share?

Yes. The shared profile is designed to make your contact details easy to keep without manually retyping them.
